What's happening to Stocky

Shopify is retiring Stocky, its stock-management app, on August 31, 2026.

Some of Stocky's functionality is moving into Shopify's admin natively — Shopify's own migration path is to use these built-in purchase order and receiving features going forward.

Merchants who built their inbound-stock workflow around Stocky's receiving and label printing need to choose and set up a replacement before the deadline, so nothing breaks when Stocky is turned off.

Migration checklist

  1. Export your data from Stocky before August 31, 2026.
  2. Recreate your open purchase orders — ReceiveFlow supports CSV import on Starter and Pro.
  3. Test a receiving workflow end to end on a phone or tablet before you rely on it.
  4. Print a test label to confirm your label stock (Dymo 30334 or thermal 2x1 / 2.25x1.25) works with your printer.
  5. Keep your Stocky exports as an archive in case you need historical records.

FAQ

When is Stocky shutting down?
Shopify is retiring Stocky on August 31, 2026. Some of its features are moving into Shopify's admin natively, but merchants who rely on Stocky's receiving and label-printing workflow need to choose a replacement before the deadline.
Do I need Shopify POS Pro to use ReceiveFlow?
No. ReceiveFlow's mobile receiving and barcode scanning work in the browser on any phone or tablet, without a POS Pro subscription. You can scan with the camera, scan a photo, use a keyboard-wedge barcode scanner, or enter barcodes manually.
Can I import purchase orders?
Yes. CSV import for purchase orders is available on the Starter ($15/mo) and Pro ($29/mo) plans, both of which include a 14-day free trial.
What label sizes are supported?
ReceiveFlow generates labels as PDF for Dymo 30334 and thermal 2x1 and 2.25x1.25 sizes, so no printer driver is needed.
What does ReceiveFlow cost?
Free is $0/month for up to 3 open purchase orders, with CSV export included on every plan. Starter is $15/month for unlimited purchase orders plus CSV import. Pro is $29/month and adds multi-location receiving, expected restock dates, and receipt history. Paid plans include a 14-day free trial.
Can I export my data if I leave?
Yes. CSV export of your purchase order data is free on every plan, including Free, so you can keep an archive whether or not you continue using the app.
