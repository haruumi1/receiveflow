Your first delivery

Before you start

ReceiveFlow keeps its own POs. It does not import native Shopify POs or Transfers. Check that this separate workflow fits your store.

Creating a PO does not change stock. Confirming receipt updates Shopify inventory. Use a small genuine delivery or a development store. Do not confirm fictitious quantities just to test labels.

1. Create a purchase order

Open ReceiveFlow from Shopify admin, choose New PO, search your existing catalog, add products and enter ordered quantities. Add your reference and supplier, then save.

2. Receive what arrived

Open the PO and enter only quantities physically received. Check the destination location before confirming. Partial deliveries leave the remaining quantity open. If the result is unclear, check the PO and inventory before trying again.

3. Check and print labels

Open the PDF and check product details, quantities, label stock, printer scaling and paper settings. Print a small sample first. Labels can reflect cumulative quantities received on the PO, not just today's delivery. Check before printing to avoid duplicates.

Verify Shopify inventory at the receiving location. Export the PO to CSV for your records.

Which plan do you need?

  • Free: up to 3 open POs, receiving, label PDFs and CSV export.
  • Starter: $15/month, unlimited open POs and CSV import.
  • Pro: $29/month, Starter features plus multi-location receiving, expected-restock dates and receipt history.

Paid plans have a 14-day trial. The manual steps above work on Free; no paid plan is needed just to follow this guide.
